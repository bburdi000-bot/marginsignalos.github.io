Statement of Independence & Neutrality

Independence is the product.

Our verdict is only worth something if it isn't ours to bias. So we made our independence a rule we publish — and one you can hold us to.

The standard we hold ourselves to

Independence for anyone judging high-risk AI is now law — and long-settled practice everywhere it matters:

EU AI Act, Article 31

A body assessing high-risk AI must be independent of any operator with an economic interest in it — and may not advise the parties it judges.

SEC / PCAOB auditors

An auditor may hold no financial interest in, and provide no conflicting services to, the entity it audits. Independence is the whole job.

The 2008 lesson

When the rated party pays for — and can shop — its own rating, the rating stops meaning anything. We never want to be that.

The rule (non-negotiable)

1 · No financial entanglement with the parties we measure.

No equity, board seats, lending, or paid consulting from any party whose AI systems, agents, or outcomes we measure — or could be asked to measure. That includes operators and deployers, AI model vendors, BPO/CX providers, and insurers underwriting the risks we attribute. If such a party is ever involved in funding, it is capped below any control threshold, given no board seat and no methodology information-rights, and disclosed publicly.

2 · Buyer-pays, not issuer-pays.

We charge the side that consumes the verdict — the enterprise, the insurer, the regulator. Where a measured party must fund a specific assessment, the fee is fixed, published, and paid regardless of the result, so no fee is ever contingent on the outcome.

3 · We verify the system and the vendor — never your team.

Our role is a shield for the buyer, not an indictment of their people. The independent read is what makes their numbers hold up under scrutiny.

4 · A structural firewall.

Our governing documents carry an independence covenant. Methodology is set by an independent committee, published, and versioned. Funding sources are disclosed.

5 · No fabricated results. No undisclosed conflicts. Ever.

We do not claim clients or results we do not have, and we disclose any relationship a reasonable party would want to know.

Scope & review

This policy binds the company, its founder, employees, contractors, and advisors. It is reviewed at least annually and published here. Material changes are versioned and dated.
