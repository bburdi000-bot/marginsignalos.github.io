Core Competencies

  • Independent verification & validation (IV&V) for AI-enabled and autonomous systems operating in regulated and mission-critical settings.
  • AI test, evaluation, verification & validation (TEVV) — model and output validation, performance and reliability assessment.
  • Fault detection & cross-party fault attribution across multi-vendor AI and inference pipelines.
  • AI governance, risk management & compliance monitoring — conformance against organizational and regulatory requirements, with audit-grade reporting.
  • Operational AI evaluation — conversational AI, customer-experience automation, and high-volume operational decision systems.

Differentiators

Structurally independent. A vendor-neutral assurance function with no stake in the models under evaluation — the auditor, not the builder. The role independent auditors hold in financial reporting, applied to autonomous AI.

Patent-pending methods. Multiple provisional patent applications filed on the verification and fault-apportionment methods, held by the company.

Operator credibility. Founded by a 15-year enterprise customer-operations and systems-operations practitioner — assurance built from how these systems actually run, not theory.

Built in production. Enterprise-grade analytics and AI-evaluation tooling designed and operated inside a Fortune 50 operations environment.

Relevant Experience

Fifteen years designing and running large-scale customer-operations, quality-assurance, and resolution programs across insurance, healthcare, ITSM, and BPO environments in regulated industries.

Designed and deployed operational-intelligence and outlier-detection tooling — agent and supervisor performance analytics, churn intelligence — on enterprise data platforms in a Fortune 50 setting.

Active evaluation under executed mutual NDA with a leading industry standards and certification organization in the customer-experience domain; warm engagement underway with public-sector AI leadership.
