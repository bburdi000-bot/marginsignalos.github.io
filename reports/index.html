Published Verification Reports

The IVR series takes claims that are widely repeated in the AI market and checks each one against the primary source — the statute, the regulator, the official audit record. Every verdict is sourced and stated plainly: VERIFIED, PARTIALLY VERIFIED, or NOT VERIFIED.

Two disciplines govern every report. We verify claims, not claimants — no company is named, in praise or in criticism. And precision beats posture: the goal is the exact position, nuance included, because an overcorrection is just a new error. Corrections to any finding are welcome and are published.
