The scrutiny is already scheduled

Multistate exam

A coordinated, multistate regulatory examination of insurers' AI practices is underway now, running through September 2026.

NAIC bulletin

The NAIC model bulletin on insurers' use of AI — adopted across roughly two dozen states — expects governance, vendor oversight, and validation of AI-driven decisions.

Old doctrine

Independent validation is already insurance doctrine: actuarial opinions on reserves, model validation, audited financials. AI is the newest entry on that list, not an exception to it.

"The vendor's dashboard says it works" has never satisfied an examiner in any of those domains. The question isn't whether AI-touched outcomes get verified — it's who is structurally allowed to verify them.
